# Drink illustrations and references

The 29 SVGs include bubble tea, iced drinks, mugs, cocktail glasses, fridge cans, water, and the menu scene. Strawberry matcha and cherry lemonade use the kitchen illustrations in assets/art/kitchen/.

The can illustrations are decorative flavor sketches. Product names and official links identify the drinks on the menu.

Flavor descriptions and caffeine amounts for the Monster cans come from Monster Energy’s official UK product pages, checked October 25, 2026: [Mixxd Punch](https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-gb/energy-drinks/juiced-monster/mixxd-punch/), [Pacific Punch](https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-gb/energy-drinks/juiced-monster/pacific-punch/), [Pipeline Punch](https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-gb/energy-drinks/juiced-monster/pipeline-punch/), [Mango Loco](https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-gb/energy-drinks/juiced-monster/mango-loco/), and [Ultra Rosá](https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-gb/energy-drinks/monster-ultra/zero-sugar-ultra-rosa/). Pepsi Max Cherry and Mango link to [Carlsberg Britvic’s Pepsi Max page](https://carlsbergbritvic.co.uk/products/pepsi/pepsi-max/), the UK bottler. Amounts can vary by country.

Artwork script: _tools/make_drinks_art.js. The menu data is in assets/js/drinks-data.js; node _tools/render_drinks_notes.js updates assets/drinks/MENU.md.

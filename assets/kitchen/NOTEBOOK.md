# My little kitchen ♡

My food diary: comfort dinners, something sweet, and two pretty drinks.

## Teriyaki chicken rice bowl

My comfort dinner

Sticky chicken, fluffy rice, and a glossy sweet-savory sauce. My idea of a very good bowl.

*A little extra sauce for the rice. I have priorities.*

### Why it gets a page

I love sweet-savory Japanese-inspired food, especially in a big rice bowl. This is exactly the sort of dinner I’d choose when I want something comforting.

### My favorite part

The rice catches all that glossy sauce. I’d want broccoli on the side and a little extra sauce over everything.

My ideal dinner comes in a bowl.

## Chicken katsu curry

My Japanese food obsession

Golden, oven-crisp chicken with a mellow curry sauce and a little mountain of rice.

*Crispy chicken and curry sauce. A very persuasive combination.*

### What wins me over

Crispy chicken, warm curry sauce, and rice. I love the combination of a crunchy coating and a soft, cozy sauce.

### My perfect plate

I’d choose a mellow curry and keep some of the chicken crisp at the edges. This gets a very enthusiastic place in my Japanese-food obsession.

I have a very soft spot for crispy chicken.

## Creamy garlic chicken pasta

My cozy evening favorite

A creamy little tangle of pasta with golden chicken, garlic, and Parmesan.

*I’d happily give garlic and Parmesan their own page.*

### My cozy evening choice

I want creamy pasta with plenty of garlic, golden chicken, and Parmesan. The sort of bowl I’d settle down with after a long day.

### The bit I’m here for

A silky sauce that clings to the pasta, with a little lemon to brighten it up. I’d be very happy with extra Parmesan at the table.

Garlic and Parmesan have my attention.

## Sweet chili chicken noodles

My quick and delicious answer

Glossy noodles, colorful vegetables, and enough sweet heat to keep things interesting.

*When I can’t decide what I want, noodles are a very convincing answer.*

### My noodle mood

I like a little sweet heat, lots of noodles, and colorful vegetables mixed through. This is what I’d choose when I’m hungry and indecisive.

### The combination I love

Chicken, sweet chili, lime, and a bit of crunch. I want a glossy bowl with enough sauce to reach every last noodle.

A good noodle bowl can settle a lot of dinner debates.

## Crispy chicken wraps

My easy lunch idea

Crunchy oven-baked chicken, fresh salad, and a creamy little garlic sauce.

*A warm wrap, a crunchy filling, and a very good lunch.*

### My kind of lunch

Crispy chicken, fresh salad, and a creamy garlic sauce, all tucked into a wrap. I like lunch that gives me something to look forward to.

### What makes it lovely

The crunch is the whole point for me. I’d want plenty of lettuce, a warm tortilla, and a little garlic sauce for dipping.

I am absolutely choosing the garlic sauce.

## Chocolate fudge brownies

My something sweet

A shiny top, a soft chocolate middle, and very little patience for the cooling part.

*Fudgy middle, chewy edges. That’s my brownie.*

### My chocolate opinion

I like a brownie with a shiny top and a soft, fudgy middle. Dark chocolate gets my vote, especially with a few richer chocolate pieces inside.

### My perfect little treat

A brownie and a warm drink would make an ordinary afternoon feel much nicer. I’d choose the corner piece for those chewy edges.

My vote goes to the corner piece. ♡

## Strawberry matcha latte

My pretty little drink

Strawberry at the bottom, creamy milk in the middle, and a soft green matcha cloud on top.

Pink and green really do make a very good argument.

## Cherry lemonade

My fruity favorite

Cherry-red, lemony, cold, and completely ready for a pretty glass.

My cherry obsession has found the drinks menu. Obviously.

## Kitchen confessions

### My dinner logic

Chicken is the answer to an unreasonable number of my dinner questions.

### My dangerous phrase

“Just a little more garlic” is how I make decisions. It is rarely the end of the conversation.

### My musical timer

I need an actual timer. “One more song” is not a reliable unit of cooking time.

### My presentation standards

A pretty bowl and a sprinkle of something green can do a lot for my confidence.

### My baking negotiations

I can improvise dinner. Brownies would like me to read the measurements, please.

### My dramatic moment

If I forget the garnish, I reserve the right to call it rustic and carry on.

## Reference accuracy

Photos illustrate a color, texture, or styling idea. The four color scraps identify differences: the blue reference is brighter than the near-black target, and the midnight reference has more visible violet sections. The current-hair note identifies its photograph as another model with longer hair, used for texture inspiration. No image is recolored or presented as Iris, an exact prediction, or a transformation. The original reference collection remains in the folded inspiration folder.

## Maintenance wording

The brief color note explains that a dark starting shade affects the result, brighter shades may need lightening, and colored tones need refreshing. General background was checked against professional color resources:

- [Wella Professionals: lilac hair ideas](https://blog.wella.com/gb/lilac-hair-color-ideas-and-formulas) — lightened bases for pastel lilac and silver-lilac results.
- [Wella Professionals: silver hair](https://blog.wella.com/gb/silver-hair-colour-trend) — lightening and maintenance for silver shades.
- [Hair.com, L’Oréal Professional Products Division: blue-black hair](https://www.hair.com/blue-black-hair-ideas.html) — depth, variation, and ongoing cool-tone/root maintenance.

The hair diary and photo folder run entirely in the browser. They send no reference selections to a server or analytics service.

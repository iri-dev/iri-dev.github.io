# Bookshelf illustrations and references

Twelve mood postcards accompany the book notes. The illustrations use scenery and objects associated with each story; publisher credits below refer to the games and stories.

Artwork script: _tools/make_collection_art.js.

## Collection

### Celeste

- Type: Game
- Illustration: `celeste.svg`
- Sketch: A snowy mountain beneath a moon and stars, with a tiny winding trail.
- [Official title page](https://www.celestegame.com/)
- Game/story credit: © Maddy Makes Games / Extremely OK Games

A little pixel-art game about climbing a mountain, except somehow it's about so much more than that. I love the music, the colors, and the idea that something so small can carry so much heart.

### Genshin Impact

- Type: Game
- Illustration: `genshin.svg`
- Sketch: Soft green hills, a winding path, flowers, and a compass.
- [Official title page](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/)
- Game/story credit: © All rights reserved by COGNOSPHERE. Other properties belong to their respective owners.

A beautiful world, gorgeous music, and endless places to explore. Sometimes I don't even need a quest. I just want to wander around and enjoy everything.

### Wuthering Waves

- Type: Game
- Illustration: `wuwa.svg`
- Sketch: Blue waves, distant hills, a pale moon, and a few birds.
- [Official title page](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/)
- Game/story credit: © KURO TECHNOLOGY (HONG KONG) CO., LIMITED. All rights reserved.

A beautiful world with combat that actually makes me pay attention. I love the contrast between peaceful exploration and suddenly having to fight for my life.

### Hollow Knight

- Type: Game
- Illustration: `hollow.svg`
- Sketch: A moonlit cave with soft pools of light and tiny stars.
- [Official title page](https://www.hollowknight.com/)
- Game/story credit: © Team Cherry

Dark little worlds, gorgeous artwork, mysterious characters, and a soundtrack that makes everything feel more magical. Exactly my kind of atmosphere.

### Terraria

- Type: Game
- Illustration: `terraria.svg`
- Sketch: A leafy tree above a grassy hillside and a little underground crystal.
- [Official title page](https://terraria.org/)
- Game/story credit: © Re-Logic

Give me a world full of things to discover and the freedom to build whatever I want, and suddenly several hours have disappeared.

### Stardew Valley

- Type: Game
- Illustration: `stardew.svg`
- Sketch: Rolling green fields, wildflowers, a fence, and a winding path.
- [Official title page](https://www.stardewvalley.net/)
- Game/story credit: © ConcernedApe

A tiny farm, flowers everywhere, cozy music, and absolutely no reason to rush. Sometimes I want a game that just lets me exist somewhere lovely.

### Steins;Gate

- Type: Anime series
- Illustration: `steinsgate.svg`
- Sketch: A vintage clock face floating above soft ripples of time.
- [Official title page](https://kadokawa-animation.jp/lineup/steinsgate)
- Game/story credit: ©2011 5pb./Nitroplus 未来ガジェット研究所

### Violet Evergarden

- Type: Anime series
- Illustration: `violet.svg`
- Sketch: A cream envelope, a wax seal, and little lavender flowers.

- Game/story credit: © Kana Akatsuki, Kyoto Animation / Violet Evergarden Production Committee

Some stories don't need huge battles or complicated twists. Sometimes all it takes is beautiful animation and a story about learning to understand people.

### Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

- Game/story credit: © Kanehito Yamada, Tsukasa Abe / SHOGAKUKAN / Frieren Project

### Outer Wilds

### NieR:Automata

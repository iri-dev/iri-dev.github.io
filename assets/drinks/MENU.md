# My little drink bar ♡

Bubble tea, pretty little drinks, and a suspicious amount of cherry.

## My bubble tea corner

### Brown sugar milk tea

Milk tea · Brown sugar · Tapioca pearls

I love the creamy milk tea, brown sugar, and chewy pearls together. This is the one I’d put at the top of my bubble tea menu.

*Those caramel stripes down the side of the cup are half the reason. The pearls are the other half.*

### Taro milk tea

Taro · Milk tea · Tapioca pearls

Sweet, creamy, a little nutty, and lilac. I like that this feels like a dessert drink with a very good color palette.

*A purple drink was always going to end up on my menu.*

### Strawberry milk tea

Strawberry · Milk tea · Tapioca pearls

I’d choose this for the sweet strawberry milk and chewy pearls. Soft pink, creamy, and very much my sort of treat.

*Pink and chewy is a combination I take very seriously.*

### Mango popping boba fruit tea

Green tea · Mango · Popping boba

I like the idea of bright mango tea with little bursts of fruit. This is my pick when I want something juicy and refreshing.

*Popping boba is basically confetti you can drink.*

## Matcha and pretty drinks

### Iced strawberry matcha latte

Strawberry · Milk · Matcha

I love the pink strawberry layer beneath the milk and green matcha. The fruity sweetness makes this especially tempting.

*Pink and green really do make a very good argument.*

### Iced vanilla matcha latte

Matcha · Vanilla · Milk

I’d choose vanilla matcha when I want something creamy and gently sweet. I love those green swirls through the milk.

*Vanilla makes matcha taste like a little dessert. I’m fine with that.*

### Iced chai latte

Chai tea · Cinnamon · Milk

I like cinnamon, cardamom, and that warm spiced flavor, even in a cold glass. This would be my cozy choice on a warm day.

*It’s a sweater in a glass, except it’s iced, so I can have it in July.*

### Iced vanilla latte

Espresso · Vanilla · Milk

I like a creamy iced coffee with a soft vanilla sweetness. A slow morning and a tall glass of this sound lovely together.

*I love watching the espresso fall through the milk. It’s the little things.*

## My fruity favorites

### Cherry lemonade

Cherry · Lemon · Still or sparkling

Cherry is one of my favorite flavors, so this gets the big spot on my menu. I’d choose it cold, lemony, and full of ice.

*My cherry obsession has found the drinks menu. Obviously.*

### Strawberry lemonade

Strawberry · Lemon · Sugar

I like the sweet strawberry and sharp lemon together. This is the pink glass I’d choose for a sunny afternoon.

*If a drink is pink, I’m already halfway convinced.*

### Peach iced tea

Black tea · Peach · Lemon

I’d choose this for the gentle peach flavor and cold black tea. Lots of ice, a tall glass, and somewhere shady to sit.

*This is exactly what I want on the hottest day of the year.*

### Raspberry sparkling lemonade

Raspberry · Lemon · Sparkling water

I love a tart berry flavor with a little fizz. This is what I’d choose when I want an ordinary afternoon to feel a bit special.

*The bubbles make it feel like an occasion, even on a Tuesday.*

## My cozy cupboard

### Classic hot chocolate

Cocoa · Milk · Marshmallows

I like a rich chocolate mug with marshmallows on top. This belongs beside a blanket and something good to watch.

*Marshmallows are not optional. I’ve thought about it.*

### White hot chocolate

White chocolate · Milk · Vanilla

I’d choose this when I want something especially sweet and creamy. White chocolate and vanilla make a very persuasive pair.

*This is dessert pretending to be a drink, and I support it.*

### Warm vanilla milk

Milk · Vanilla · Honey

I like the idea of a warm vanilla mug for a quiet evening. Soft, simple, and exactly the mood I want before bed.

*For evenings when I want something warm and absolutely nothing exciting.*

### Cinnamon hot chocolate

Cocoa · Cinnamon · Whipped cream

I love chocolate with a warm cinnamon flavor and a little whipped cream. This is the mug I’d choose for a cold evening.

*Cinnamon makes everything feel like a cozy little holiday.*

## After Hours ♡

### Cherry Amaretto Sour

Amaretto · Cherry · Lemon

Sweet almond amaretto, tart lemon, and cherry, shaken until there’s a soft foam on top.

*If I’m choosing a signature cocktail, obviously there’s going to be cherry involved.*

#### The flavors I love

Almond, cherry, and a little sharp lemon are such a lovely combination. I’d want the cherry to come through clearly.

#### My kind of evening

A slow evening with my favorite people, a good playlist, and nowhere I need to rush off to.

Cherry gets the signature spot. Obviously. ♡

### Cosmopolitan

Vodka · Cranberry · Lime

Bright pink, a little tart, and served in a glass that does half the work.

*A pretty glass, a gorgeous color, and a little bit of attitude.*

#### What draws me in

I like the cranberry-and-citrus combination, especially with that tart edge. The gorgeous pink color is a very good bonus.

#### The mood I picture

Getting dressed up, choosing silver jewelry, and heading out for the evening. This is the glass I’d put beside that outfit.

A little pink, a little sharp, a little dressed up.

### Aviation

Gin · Violet · Lemon · Maraschino

Gin, cherry-based maraschino liqueur, lemon, and crème de violette. Floral, citrusy, and the softest shade of violet.

*A purple cocktail? I’m practically obligated to be interested.*

#### Why this is on my menu

The soft violet color caught my attention first. I love the idea of floral violet with lemon and a little cherry.

#### The little details

An elegant coupe, one cherry, and something amethyst-colored to wear. I’d choose this when I’m feeling especially fond of purple.

Purple was always going to get its own glass.

### Mojito

Rum · Mint · Lime

Fresh mint, lime, a little sugar, and white rum over crushed ice, topped with soda.

*Sometimes I just want something fresh and lovely.*

#### My refreshing choice

I like the fresh mint and lime together. This is the one I’d choose when I want a cold, bright drink with a citrusy edge.

#### Where I’d like to be

Outside on a warm evening, talking long after the sun starts going down. A tall glass and plenty of ice fit that picture perfectly.

My fresh-air-and-lime mood.

### Strawberry Daiquiri

Rum · Strawberry · Lime

Frozen strawberries, lime, and white rum blended until thick and icy.

*Strawberry, lime, and a frosty pink glass. I was always going to be interested.*

#### My fruity side

I love the strawberry-and-lime combination, especially with that thick, icy texture. This would be my cheerful summer choice.

#### What I love about it

A deep strawberry pink, a frosty glass, and a little fruit on the rim. I’m very easy to win over with a pretty fruity drink.

Strawberry pink belongs on my evening menu, too.

### Rosé

Light · Fruity · Relaxed

A pale, dry, fruity pink wine, served nice and cold.

*Some evenings call for a pretty glass of wine and absolutely no rush.*

#### My glass of wine

I’d choose a light, dry rosé with a fruity flavor. The pale pink color and a simple, elegant glass are exactly my taste.

#### The evening I picture

A relaxed dinner, a long conversation, and no plans to hurry. This is the quietest little corner of my cabinet.

A pretty evening with absolutely no rush.

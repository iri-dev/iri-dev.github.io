why I'm making her

I wanted to drink more water, and every app that could help wanted a monthly payment first. Being reminded to look after yourself shouldn't cost money, so I made my own and gave it a heart.

things people ask me

Little questions

When can I get it?

Soon! I'm finishing her off now. Follow HydraHeart on GitHub and you'll see the first release the moment it's out.

Will it cost anything?

No. No subscription, no ads and no premium version. Everything in the app is for everyone.

Does it need the internet?

No. HydraHeart doesn't even ask for internet permission, so your drinks, moods and diary never leave your phone.

Do I have to have the cute heart?

Not at all. Focus Mode hides her face and speech bubbles and leaves a calm, simple tracker. You can switch whenever you like, and she won't take it personally.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not right now. HydraHeart is an Android app.

Got an idea for Hydra, like a drink, a theme or something she should say? Tell me. She's still growing.

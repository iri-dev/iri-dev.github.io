Welcome to my little room

the website

Hi! ♡ I wanted my website to feel less like a CV and more like somewhere you could actually visit. Somewhere with a bit of me in it. So I made it a room.

a tiny tour

The pinboard is where you'll find out about me. The shelves hold my favourite things. The laptop has everything I've made, the diary on my desk is where you are right now, and the little pocket of letters by the window is how to say hello.

things to try

Click the lamp and the whole room goes cosy. At night, the window takes wishes. The cat on the bed is asleep, but she doesn't mind a pat. And if you happen to know a certain old video game code… try it. ✿

what you won't find

I don’t run ads or site analytics. Even the fonts live on this website. Spotify is the exception: its player connects to Spotify only when you choose to load it, and then its own privacy policy applies.

a room you can visit, not a page that watches you.

Everything is made by hand, and all of it is open for you to read. If you spot a bug, it's probably mine, and I'd love to hear about it.

make yourself at home, Iri ♡

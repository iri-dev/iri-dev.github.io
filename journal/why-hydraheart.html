Why I'm making HydraHeart

making things

HydraHeart started with a very small goal: I wanted to drink more water. Not a big life change, just a little habit I kept meaning to build.

So I went looking for an app to help, and there were plenty. The catch was always the same, though. The nice parts sat behind a monthly payment.

a reminder to drink water should not be a premium feature.

I'm a developer. I know apps take time and work to make. But some things feel like they should just be there for everyone, and this is one of them.

so I made my own

I started building something just for me, with no ads and nothing locked away. And the more I worked on it, the more I wanted it to feel less like a spreadsheet and more like a friend.

say hi to Hydra

Hydra is a little glass heart. Every drink you log pours into her in its own colour, her face changes as your day goes on, and as you level up she collects little treasures: a necklace, some sparkles, and eventually a crown.

If you'd rather keep it simple, you can hide her face and just see your numbers. She won't take it personally.

free, for everyone

Somewhere along the way I realised I didn't want to keep her to myself. So HydraHeart is going to be free: no ads, no account, no tracking. It doesn't even need the internet to work.

Taking care of yourself is hard enough without a paywall in the way. 💧

love, Iri ♡

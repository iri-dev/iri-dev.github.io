Hello, I'm Iris
(but call me Iri)

life

Hi! ♡ If you've used anything I've made, you probably know me as Iri, or as iri-dev on GitHub. For a long time, that's all I shared.

Then I started asking more of people. WardenOne sits in your browser and looks after your passwords and your downloads. HydraHeart wants to be part of your day. That's a lot of trust to ask from someone who's only a nickname.

So, nice to meet you properly: my name is Iris Molina.

so what do I call you?

Iri, please! It's just Iris with the s chopped off. I've always thought it was cute, and it's the name that feels most like me. (It's pronounced eye-ree.)

If you see Iris Molina, Iri or iri-dev around the internet, don't worry. They're all me.

and what stays private

Sharing my name doesn't mean sharing everything. Where I am and my day-to-day life stay mine. I think that's a healthy balance.

open about who I am and what I make. private about the rest.

Thank you for being here, and for trusting the things I make. It means more than you might think.

love, Iri ♡

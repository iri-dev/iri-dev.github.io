My privacy note ♡

I want this little room to feel comfortable to visit. Here’s what stays in your browser, and what happens if you choose an outside service.

Updated October 24, 2026

A quiet little website

I don’t add advertising, site analytics, tracking pixels, accounts, or contact forms. My illustrations, reference photos, fonts, and guitar notes are hosted with the site. I don’t send nail designs, guitar notes, or gallery choices to a server.

The things my browser remembers

I use local storage to remember the light or dark theme and the arcade’s best scores. These preferences stay in that browser; I don’t receive them. Clearing this site’s browser data removes them. The site still works if storage is blocked, but it may forget those preferences.

When I invite Spotify in

I leave Spotify unloaded until you choose load Spotify player on My World or in my music corner. That choice connects your browser to Spotify and loads its official playlist player. Spotify may receive your IP address, browser or device details, and information about how you use its player. It may also use cookies and similar technologies under its own policies.

I don’t read your Spotify account, receive your listening history, or store a copy of a playlist’s track list. Playback is provided by Spotify, and its terms, privacy policy, and cookie policy apply to that service.

I include a close Spotify player button to remove the player and stop its playback. Closing it doesn’t erase information already sent or cookies already stored. Those can be managed through your browser and Spotify’s own privacy controls. I also include direct playlist links, so using the embedded player is optional.

Hosting and saying hello

I publish this site through GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information, such as IP addresses and requests, to host and secure its services under its privacy statement. I don’t run visitor analytics on top of that hosting.

If you follow an outside link, that service’s policies apply. If you choose to email or message me, I receive the message and the contact information you send through that service. For a privacy question, you can reach me through my contact page.

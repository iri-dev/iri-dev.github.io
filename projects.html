why WardenOne?

The internet can feel like a bit of a minefield, and I wanted one simple thing that quietly looks after you while you browse. You stay in charge, too: anything you don't like, you can turn off.

why HydraHeart?

I wanted to drink more water, and every app that could help wanted a monthly payment first. That felt wrong to me. So I made my own, gave it a heart, and decided it should be free for everyone.

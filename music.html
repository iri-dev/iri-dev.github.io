      A few notes before I make some noise ♪

      I can tap a fret to hold and play a note, or brush across the strings over the body to strum. My chord scribbles put all six strings in place for me. The little numbers on the strings show which frets I'm holding; × means that string is muted.

      On a smaller screen, I can open a closer look at the frets and scroll sideways along the guitar. A tap plays a note; a vertical brush across the strings plays a strum.

      With the guitar focused, 1–6 pick high E through low E. Space strums down; Shift + Space strums up. ↑ and ↓ choose a string; ← and → move one fret and play it.

      For a melody, the top keyboard row Q W E R T Y U I O P [ ] \ plays frets 0–12 on my selected string. Escape hushes both guitars and stops the example tune.

      A tiny song to try: I press 1 to choose high E, then play Q Q I I P P I, followed by Y Y T T E E Q. That's the opening of the traditional melody Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star. With a mouse or my fingertips, the same frets are 0 0 7 7 9 9 7 · 5 5 4 4 2 2 0, all on the top string. My last note in each phrase gets a little longer to ring.

      My little browser guitars use locally hosted recordings of guitar notes, with standard E–A–D–G–B–E tuning. These aren't recordings of me playing. Acoustic sounds: Meta Steel Guitar; electric sounds: FreePats FSBS. Both CC0. Sound credits.

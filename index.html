My projects About me

I'm a software engineering and cyber security graduate. I build things that make the internet a little safer and everyday life a little easier, and I build them the way I'd want them built for me: free, private, no accounts, no ads, nothing quietly tracking you. Every line is open to read, so you never have to take my word for it.

I'm one person, not a company, and I'd rather earn your trust slowly than ask for it up front.

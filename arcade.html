how to play: Fill up Hydra

Move Hydra to catch the falling drinks. Water fills her up the most, then tea and bubble tea.

Coffee and energy drinks barely count, and they give her the jitters. Five beans of caffeine and she's done for the day! Caffeine wears off slowly, so space them out.

Fill her to the top and she reaches her goal, earns a crown, and the drinks start falling faster.

← → or A D to move · drag on the screen · P to pause

how to play: Squash the bugs

Bugs crawl in from the edges and start chewing on my code. Tap them before they finish! Three broken lines and the build fails.

The little gold stars are features, not bugs. Squash one and something breaks. (It's not a bug, it's a feature.)

Every ten bugs you squash, I fix a broken line for you.

Click or tap to squash · P to pause
